Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Can my child easily uninstall or disable ChildGuard AI? ▼

No. ChildGuard AI is enrolled as an Android Device Administrator and registers an Accessibility Service lock. Attempting to deactivate device admin requires a parental password, and the service immediately dispatches an emergency tamper notification to parents before survival daemons re-arm the protection.

How does the silent distress SOS trigger work? ▼

If a child is in distress, they do not need to unlock the phone or open an app. Rapidly clicking the physical Volume Down button 3 times within 2 seconds instantly captures current GPS coordinates and battery level, delivers a subtle stealth vibration to let the child know help was called, and broadcasts a critical emergency distress alarm to parents.

How does ChildGuard AI prevent battery drain? ▼

ChildGuard AI utilizes Google Play Services Fused Location Provider with adaptive movement detection. When the child is stationary (e.g. at school or home), GPS sampling shifts to low-power geofence triggers, keeping hourly battery usage well below 1.8%.

Is my child's location or chat data sold or shared? ▼

Never. ChildGuard AI complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. Chat inspection occurs strictly on-device without uploading personal conversations. Furthermore, historical telemetry is automatically purged every 30 days, and parents can completely self-host their own Docker backend.
